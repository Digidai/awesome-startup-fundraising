# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. It's a curated map of tools and resources for founders raising pre-seed to Series A, maintained by the team at [Evalyze](https://www.evalyze.ai/). Every entry, including Evalyze's own, has to meet the same bar.

---

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository and create a branch.
2. Add one resource to the most relevant section of `README.md`, in alphabetical order.
3. Open a pull request and fill in the template, including the disclosure line.

To report a broken link, a shutdown or a rebrand, open an issue instead. Those are some of the most useful contributions.

---

## What gets accepted

A resource is added when all of these are true:

- It's relevant to raising money for an early-stage startup.
- It's live on its own domain (not a free subdomain, a staging link or a demo running on sample data).
- It has been active in the last six months (updates, posts or releases).
- Pricing is clear, or there's a free tier.
- It isn't already listed. One entry per product, in the section it fits best. A company with separate products can have one entry for each.

The **Most Active Early-Stage VCs** and **Active Angel Investors** sections are maintained by the editors from public deal data, so they aren't open to submissions. If someone listed there looks inactive or a description is out of date, open an issue.

## What gets closed

- Descriptions with marketing language ("best," "leading," "#1," "revolutionary") or numbers the tool's own website doesn't show.
- Affiliate or referral links and codes.
- Several resources in one pull request.
- Pull requests that don't say whether you work on or are paid by the tool. Disclosing doesn't count against you, and most additions come from the people who build the tools. Not disclosing does.

---

## Format

```md
- [**Tool Name**](https://example.com/) → Short, neutral description of what it does, 20 words or fewer.
```

Example:

```md
- [**Crunchbase**](https://www.crunchbase.com/) → Database of companies, investors, and funding rounds for market and investor research.
```

---

## Review

If something on the list stops meeting these rules (it shuts down, rebrands or goes stale), it gets updated or removed.
